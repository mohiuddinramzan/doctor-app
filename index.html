Frequently asked questions

How do I book an appointment?

Tap "Book an Appointment", choose new or existing patient, pick a chamber, date and time slot, fill in your details and confirm payment. You'll get an appointment ID and QR code instantly.

What is the consultation fee?

New patient visits are ৳1,500. The fee is shown again before you pay, broken down clearly.

What is the follow-up fee?

৳800, valid for 15 days after your last visit with a prescription. After that it's treated as a new visit.

How can I cancel an appointment?

Open "My Appointments", find your booking and tap Cancel. It's reflected immediately in the doctor's queue.

Where is the chamber?

Green Life Heart Center, Dhanmondi (Sat/Sun/Tue/Thu) and City Medical Chamber, Banani (Mon/Wed). Maps links are on the chamber cards above.

How early should I arrive?

15 minutes before your slot time is enough — serials move roughly on schedule but can shift by a few patients.

Can I book for a family member?

Yes. Add them under Family Members in your Patient Dashboard, then select them when booking.
